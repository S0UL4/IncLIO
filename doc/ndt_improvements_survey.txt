CONTEXT
───────
NDT has had ~15 years of incremental improvements + one major reformulation.
This doc catalogs the families of work and rates them for integration into
IncLIO (P2D-NDT scan-to-map + IESKF, Mid-360 + IMU, real-time 10 Hz).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Empirical baseline — Magnusson et al., ICRA 2009
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Evaluation of 3D Registration Reliability and Speed — A Comparison of ICP and NDT"

Key findings (Kvarntorp mine, 441 starts, 150m loop):
  - Convergence basin (loose/strict translation):
      ICP                 30% / 13%
      plain NDT           77% / 37%
      trilinear NDT       95% / 95%
  - Speed:    plain NDT ~3-5x faster than ICP
              trilinear NDT ~5x slower than plain NDT (still ~ICP)
  - Loop accuracy (~150m):
      plain NDT 2.26m   ICP 2.97m   trilinear NDT 3.99m
  - NDT failures can be CATASTROPHIC (large rotation error).
    ICP failures are graceful (rotation OK, translation slides).
  - Failures are non-monotonic: smaller initial error sometimes fails where
    larger error succeeds. Root cause: discontinuities in the score function
    at voxel boundaries.

Trilinear NDT score function (their fix for the discontinuity):
    s(p) = -1/c * sum_{b=1..8} w(x', q_b) * exp(-1/2 * (x'-q_b)^T C_b^-1 (x'-q_b))
    where {q_b, C_b} are the 8 cells around x', w() is trilinear weight.

Note re: corridor accuracy: trilinear NDT actually WORSENED loop accuracy
because it shortened featureless tunnels. The smoother score lets the
optimizer slide further along the degenerate corridor axis. This is the
exact failure mode the per-axis blending plan addresses.


================================================================================
  FAMILY 1 — Algorithmic core changes (what residual to optimize)
================================================================================

D2D-NDT (Distribution-to-Distribution) ★★★★★
   Stoyanov, Magnusson, Andreasson, Lilienthal — IJRR 2012.
   "Fast and accurate scan registration through minimization of the distance
    between compact 3D NDT representations."

   Most important "improve NDT" paper after the original.
   Voxelizes the SCAN side too. Residuals become per-voxel, not per-point.
   10-50x fewer residuals, density-invariant weighting, rotation enters
   covariance not just mean. SEE d2d_ndt_integration.txt FOR FULL DETAIL.

VGICP (Voxelized GICP) ★★★★
   Koide, Yokozuka, Oishi, Banno — ICRA 2021.
   "Voxelized GICP for fast and accurate 3D point cloud registration."

   Not strictly NDT — sibling. Voxel-based, Gaussian-per-voxel, but uses
   GICP's plane-to-plane covariance form (C_a + R C_b R^T)^-1 instead of
   NDT's exponential. Often outperforms both NDT and GICP. Open-source
   library: small_gicp (Koide 2024). Good A/B benchmark target.

Trilinear-Interpolated NDT ★★★
   Magnusson et al., ICRA 2009 (above). Already covered.
   8-neighbor cell evaluation with trilinear weights. Smooths score-function
   discontinuities. Still applicable on top of D2D. Cost ~5x.

LiTAMIN / LiTAMIN2 ★★★
   Yokozuka, Oishi, Thompson, Banno — ICRA 2020/2021.
   NDT-like Gaussians but symmetric KL-divergence cost. Tuned for real time,
   better numerical conditioning. Worth reading for cost-function design.

Color / Intensity NDT ★★
   Magnusson PhD 2009; Stoyanov follow-ups.
   Adds intensity to each cell's Gaussian. Mid-360 has good reflectivity —
   could disambiguate corridors with painted/reflective markings where
   geometry alone is degenerate.

================================================================================
  FAMILY 2 — Map / structure changes
================================================================================

Multi-resolution NDT ★★★★
   Magnusson PhD 2009 (cascaded 2m -> 1m -> 0.5m).
   Ulas & Temeltas, "3D multi-layered normal distribution transform," 2013.

   Cell-size hierarchy. Cheapest experiment, big basin gain.
   Cascaded version: 3 successive registrations from coarse to fine,
   each warm-starting the next. Formal multi-layered version: single score
   summing across resolutions.

Adaptive Voxel / VoxelMap ★★★★★
   Yuan et al. — RAL/ICRA 2022.
   "Efficient and Probabilistic Adaptive Voxel Mapping for Accurate Online
    LiDAR Odometry."

   Per-voxel plane fitting WITH uncertainty. Octree subdivision when a voxel
   is non-planar (lambda_min/lambda_mid > threshold). Beats FAST-LIO2 on
   most published benchmarks. Closest "modern NDT-flavored map" in the lit.

NDT-OM (Occupancy-aware NDT) ★★
   Saarinen, Andreasson, Stoyanov, Lilienthal — IJRR 2013.
   "3D normal distributions transform occupancy maps."

   Adds free-space / occupancy modeling so unobserved cells don't poison
   registration. More invasive map change. Probably not worth it before
   loop closure.

Linked-Cells / Adaptive Cells
   Magnusson PhD. Empty cells store pointer to nearest occupied cell so
   point lookups outside the surface still produce a contribution.

iVox (sparse voxel hash, FAST-LIO competitor) ★★★
   Bai et al. — RAL 2022 (Faster-LIO).
   Validates that voxel-hash maps outperform ikd-tree by 1.5-2x. IncLIO's
   NdtMap is already on this side architecturally.

================================================================================
  FAMILY 3 — Application-specific NDT
================================================================================

NDTMC (loop closure descriptor) ★★★★ (already on plan)
   Hu et al. 2023. NDT-based scan context. See loop_closure_steps.txt.

NDT-MCL (Monte Carlo Localization)
   Saarinen et al., IROS 2013.

NDT keypoints / global NDT
   Magnusson PhD. Keypoints from cells with strong eigenvalue separation.
   Useful for relocalization later.

================================================================================
  FAMILY 4 — Robustness / degeneracy (orthogonal to "improve NDT itself")
================================================================================

Solution Remapping / Degenerate-direction projection
   Zhang, Kaess, Singh — ICRA 2016.
   "On degeneracy of optimization-based state estimators for localization."
   Binary degenerate-direction detection from Hessian SVD. Per-axis blending
   (this project) generalizes it to continuous σ.

X-ICP — Localizability-aware ICP ★★★★
   Tuna, Nubert, Khattak, Hutter — T-RO 2024.
   Modern formulation. Concepts transfer directly to NDT (same SVD).

Learning-based localizability
   Nubert et al., IROS 2022.

Degeneracy-aware factors
   Hinduja, Ho, Kaess — IROS 2019.
   Same spirit at the factor-graph level — relevant once GTSAM backend exists.


================================================================================
  PRIORITY FOR INCLIO (sorted by ROI)
================================================================================

1. D2D-NDT residual                          ★★★★★  (see d2d_ndt_integration.txt)
   - 10-50x fewer residuals
   - Density-invariant, better-conditioned HTVH for blending
   - Reuses ScanLocalNdtGrid you'll build for loop closure anyway

2. Coarse-to-fine multi-resolution           ★★★★
   - Cheapest experiment, no algorithm change
   - 1.0m -> 0.5m cascade in 2 passes

3. VoxelMap-style plane-aware adaptive voxel ★★★★
   - Promote "is voxel planar?" flag from eigenvalues
   - Switch to point-to-plane residual when planar
   - Octree subdivision on non-planar voxels (bigger lift)

4. Per-axis blending                         ★★★★  (already planned, ORIGINAL idea)
   - Continuous σ blend NDT + IMU per DOF
   - Degenerate-zone state machine + recovery ramp
   - See steps_per_axis_blending.txt

5. NDTMC loop closure                        ★★★★  (already planned)
   - Phase A detection, Phase B GTSAM
   - See loop_closure_steps.txt

6. Trilinear NDT interpolation               ★★★
   - Basin widener, complementary to all above
   - Cost ~5x; consider only on first IESKF iteration

7. Robust Cauchy kernel on residuals         ★★★
   - One scalar reweighting per residual in HTVH accumulation
   - Big robustness win for free

8. VGICP A/B benchmark                       ★★★
   - Not to replace NDT, just to know if NDT is in the right ballpark
   - small_gicp (Koide 2024) is plug-and-play

9. Color / intensity NDT                     ★★
   - Mid-360 reflectivity could help in textured-but-degenerate corridors
   - Bigger commitment

10. NDT-OM (occupancy)                       ★★
    - Defer until loop closure backend exists


================================================================================
  REMINDERS
================================================================================
- Citations from training data — verify before basing decisions on them.
- The "NDT diverges in some scenes" intuition is real but nuanced. NDT
  beat ICP on accuracy AND speed in the 2009 evaluation; the failure mode
  is unpredictable rather than worse-on-average.
- Trilinear NDT can WORSEN accuracy in corridors (shortens tunnels). It
  widens basin at the cost of slipping along degenerate axes. Per-axis
  blending must be in place before adding trilinear, or you trade one
  problem for another.
- Modern LIO winners (DLIO, iG-LIO, Point-LIO, KISS-ICP) have moved away
  from pure NDT — but most use voxel+Gaussian residuals that are NDT in
  spirit. The architecture we have is the right architecture; the residual
  formulation is what to evolve.
